Viet Sidewalk Support

Out for coffee. In for an adventure. Need a hand? Start here.

How do I play?

Move left and right, JUMP over obstacles and use ACT to board, climb or drive. Watch for the scooter BEEP before bikes arrive. Sugarcane juice restores a heart, and every coffee stop saves your walk. On phones, the touch buttons sit inside the screen's safe area; landscape both ways is supported. Game controllers work too.

Where is my progress saved?

On your device only. Coffee stops, finished cities and best times are saved automatically in the game's own storage. We cannot see, recover or transfer it. Deleting the app deletes your progress. There is no cloud save.

What is free, and what is the Vietnam Tour?

Ho Chi Minh City is free. The Vietnam Tour is a one-time, lifetime purchase that unlocks the other Vietnam cities (Mui Ne, Da Lat, Nha Trang, Da Nang, Hoi An and Hanoi) and the Vietnam cities added to the campaign later. It is not a subscription, and the game has no ads.

Restore Purchase

Already bought the Vietnam Tour? Open the city map and choose RESTORE PURCHASE. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple Account or Google account you used to buy it. Restoring is free. Once restored, the cities stay open when you are offline.
