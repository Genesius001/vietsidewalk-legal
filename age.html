Viet Sidewalk Age Suitability Note

Viet Sidewalk is a kind, comedic platform game about a walk to a coffee shop. It is made for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13.

What happens in the game

  • Passing bikes and scooters can bump the walker, and a falling brick or a long drop can knock them over. Each bump costs one heart. There is no blood, injury detail or death: when the hearts run out, the walk restarts from the last coffee stop.
  • Construction workers drop bricks by accident. Nobody fights, and the player never attacks anyone.
  • A decommissioned tank and helicopter stand as museum exhibits to climb over. They never move or fire.
  • Coffee and sugarcane juice are the only drinks. There is no alcohol, tobacco, gambling, romance or sexual content, and no bad language.
  • There are no ads, chat, user-generated content, web browser, notifications or links to social media.
  • Ho Chi Minh City is free. The only purchase is the one-time Vietnam Tour, offered through the App Store or Google Play purchase screen.

Store ratings

The App Store rating is 9+ (infrequent, mild cartoon violence). Google Play shows the rating calculated by the IARC questionnaire for your region. Ratings can differ by country, so check the rating shown in your store.
