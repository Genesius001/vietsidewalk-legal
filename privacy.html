Viet Sidewalk Privacy Policy

Effective: 25 September 2026

Viet Sidewalk is a game made by Griffonix Inc. ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to information when you play Viet Sidewalk on iPhone or Android.

Summary

  • There is no account and no sign-in.
  • The game has no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • The game does not use your camera, microphone, location, photos or contacts, and it does not send notifications.
  • Your progress stays on your device. We do not receive it.
  • Purchases are processed by Apple or Google, not by us.

Information kept on your device

The game stores small files in its private app storage:

  • settings: language, music and sound on or off, difficulty and touch controls;
  • progress: your last coffee-stop checkpoint, finished cities and best times;
  • if you buy the Vietnam Tour: a note that your store account owns it, with the store's transaction reference, so the cities stay open when you are offline.

This information is used only to run the game on your device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it. On iPhone, your device's own backup (for example iCloud Backup) may include app data under your Apple account and Apple's terms; we do not receive those backups. On Android, the game excludes its data from Android app backups. There is no cloud save.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect, receive or store personal information through the game. We do not collect device identifiers, advertising identifiers, usage statistics, crash reports or location. We do not sell or share personal information, and we do not track you across apps or websites.

Apple and Google may give developers aggregated, anonymous statistics about app store activity (such as download and sales counts) under their own policies. These reports do not identify you to us.

Purchases

Ho Chi Minh City is free. The one-time Vietnam Tour purchase unlocks the other Vietnam cities. The App Store (Apple) or Google Play (Google) handles the payment under your store account and its own privacy policy. We do not receive your payment card details. The game asks the store whether your store account owns the Vietnam Tour; you can restore it with RESTORE PURCHASE on the city map using the same store account. Refunds are handled by the store, and a refunded purchase is removed from the game.

Contacting us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write, and use them only to answer you. We keep support emails only as long as needed to help you and to keep ordinary business records, then delete them. You can ask us to delete your messages at any time.

Children

Viet Sidewalk is a kind, cartoon game for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. Because the game does not collect personal information, it does not collect personal information from children.

Your choices and rights

You control the game's data on your device: delete the app to remove it. For questions about purchases or refunds, contact Apple or Google. For anything else, email us.

The only personal information we may hold is what you send us by email. We use it to answer your request. Depending on where you live (for example in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom), you may have the right to access, correct or delete that information, to object to or restrict its use, and to complain to your local data protection authority. To use these rights, email us.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update it on this page and change the effective date. If a future version of the game starts collecting information, sends notifications or shows announcements from an online source, we will update this policy and the store privacy details before that version is released.

Languages

This policy is written in English. Translations are provided for convenience; if a translation differs from the English text, the English text applies.
