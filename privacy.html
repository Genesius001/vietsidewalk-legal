Viet Sidewalk Privacy Policy

Effective: 4 November 2026

Viet Sidewalk is a game made by Griffonix Inc. ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to information when you play Viet Sidewalk on iPhone or Android.

Summary

  • There is no account and no sign-in.
  • Versions that support advertising may show Griffonix promotions, ads we place directly for other advertisers, and Google AdMob ads. Google may process data for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention when the relevant features are enabled.
  • The game does not request access to your camera, microphone, location, photos or contacts, and it does not send notifications.
  • Your progress stays on your device. We do not receive it.
  • Purchases are processed by Apple or Google, not by us. On Android, the game includes Google's Play Billing library to talk to Google Play; Google may process technical information about how billing works (see Purchases and store services).

Information kept on your device

The game stores small files in its private app storage:

  • settings: language, music and sound on or off, difficulty and touch controls;
  • progress: your last coffee-stop checkpoint, finished cities and best times;
  • if you buy the Vietnam Tour: a note that your store account owns it, with the store's transaction reference, so the cities stay open when you are offline.

Your progress and settings stay on your device; we do not upload them. The game also keeps a local record of verified purchase access for offline play. Store software exchanges purchase information with Apple or Google to process and confirm purchases, as described below. Deleting the app deletes its local data. On iPhone, your device's own backup (for example iCloud Backup) may include app data under your Apple account and Apple's terms; we do not receive those backups. On Android, the game excludes its data from Android app backups. There is no cloud save.

Information Griffonix receives

Griffonix does not operate a game account, cloud save or gameplay analytics service. We receive the information you send when you contact us. Hosting, advertising and store providers handle the information described below.

Apple and Google may give developers aggregated, anonymous statistics about app store activity (such as download and sales counts) under their own policies. These reports do not identify you to us.

Advertising

Versions with advertising support can check a campaign feed and retrieve ad images over HTTPS from GitHub Pages. The host receives connection information, such as an IP address, to deliver the content and operate the service. The game does not send it your progress, purchase reference, or a record of your ad impressions or clicks. See GitHub's privacy statement.

When network ads are enabled and you are eligible to see them, Google AdMob and the Google User Messaging Platform process advertising and consent information. Google may process IP addresses, approximate location inferred from IP, device or account identifiers, app and ad interactions, and diagnostic information for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. Network ad requests use non-personalized settings. We do not send your saves or store transaction references to AdMob. Google handles its data under the Google Privacy Policy.

Open Ads & privacy on the title screen to manage Google ad privacy choices when available. To report an ad, use its information control or Report ad under Ads & privacy, as available. Report ad opens an email draft that may include an ad reference. You decide whether to send it. Selecting an ad may open an external website or app store listing under its own privacy policy.

Purchases and store services

Ho Chi Minh City is free. The one-time Vietnam Tour purchase unlocks the other eight current cities: Mui Ne, Da Lat, Nha Trang, Da Nang, Hoi An, Hanoi and Ha Long on the mainland tour, and Phu Quoc, a bonus island. It also unlocks the Vietnam cities we add to the main campaign later.

The App Store (Apple) or Google Play (Google) handles the payment under your store account and its own privacy policy. We do not receive your payment card details. The game asks the store whether your store account owns the Vietnam Tour. The store answers with that status and a transaction reference, which the game keeps on your device. You can restore the purchase with RESTORE PURCHASE on the city map using the same store account.

On Android, the game includes Google's Play Billing library, which connects to the Google Play Store app on your device and to Google's services. Google states that this library logs information about how its functions are used (such as successes and failures) and about connection problems, to improve the library and to provide better support for errors. Those logs go to Google over an encrypted connection together with basic details about the device and the app (such as the device model, Android version, language, country, mobile network code and app version). Google handles that information under the Google Privacy Policy; we do not receive it. On iPhone, purchases use Apple's StoreKit, and Apple handles purchase information under the Apple Privacy Policy.

Refunds are handled by the store. Paid access is removed when the store confirms to the game that the purchase was refunded or revoked, or when you choose RESTORE PURCHASE and the store reports neither a purchase nor a pending one for your account. Because the game keeps verified access for offline play, a refunded purchase can stay open on a device until the store confirms its status.

Contacting us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write, and use them only to answer you. We keep support emails only as long as needed to help you and to keep ordinary business records, then delete them. You can ask us to delete your messages at any time.

Children

Viet Sidewalk is a kind, cartoon game for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. The game does not ask anyone, including children, for personal information, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices and rights

You control the game's data on your device: delete the app to remove it. For questions about purchases or refunds, contact Apple or Google. For anything else, email us.

The only personal information we may hold is what you send us by email. We use it to answer your request. Depending on where you live (for example in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom), you may have the right to access, correct or delete that information, to object to or restrict its use, and to complain to your local data protection authority. To use these rights, email us.

Changes

If our data practices change, we will update this policy and the applicable store disclosures before releasing the relevant change.
